Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats can I upload?

You can upload PDF, DOC, or DOCX files. Maximum file size is 5MB.

How many questions will I get?

You'll get exactly 10 questions: 5 MCQs based on your resume and 5 theory questions based on your selected domain.

How is the score calculated?

Final score (1-10) is based on MCQ accuracy (40%) and theory answer quality (60%) evaluated by AI.

Is voice input available?

Yes! Voice input works on Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. Voice output is available on all browsers.

Will my resume be shared publicly?

No, your resume is kept private and only used for generating personalized questions.

Can I retake the interview?

Yes! You can retake the interview with your current or a different resume anytime.
